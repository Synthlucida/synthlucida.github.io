What VEHICLE LOGBOOK does

SYNTHLUCIDA VEHICLE LOGBOOK is a free online trip logbook. You log individual trips by odometer reading, purpose (business/private), and vehicle, and the app keeps clear monthly summaries and lets you track vehicle service records too. The whole app runs directly in your browser.

What data the app collects

The app doesn't collect or send any personal data to a server. Specifically:

  • There's no registration, login, or user accounts.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used (no Google Analytics, no ad pixels).
  • No ads are shown.
  • The trips, odometer readings, and service records you enter are processed entirely in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Storage in your browser (localStorage)

The app stores the following in your browser's localStorage (locally on your device, not on a server):

  • Trip records (date, odometer, purpose, vehicle).
  • Vehicle service records.
  • The last values entered in the form.

This data never leaves your browser, and the app has no access to it from anywhere else. You can remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

Data export and import

You can export your trip and service records as PDF or CSV (e.g. for tax purposes), or as a full JSON backup, which can later be imported back the same way, for example when switching to a new device. Both export and import happen locally in your browser — the app never sends any of these files anywhere on its own; it only offers them to you for download, or processes a file you upload yourself.

External resources

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Loading the page therefore sends a standard request to Google's servers, which is common practice for most sites using custom fonts. Aside from that, the app loads no other external scripts or tracking code.

Accuracy and use for tax purposes

The app serves as supporting documentation for trips and servicing. For tax and accounting purposes we recommend backing up your records regularly (JSON export) and consulting an accountant where needed.

Found a problem?

If you run into a bug or have any question about privacy or how the app works, please reach out via the contact form on synthlucida.com. We'll do our best to sort it out quickly.
