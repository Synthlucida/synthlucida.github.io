Privacy Policy

App: SYNTHLUCIDA VEHICLE LOGBOOK · synthlucida.com/denik-vozidla-en.html · Last updated: Sep 23, 2026
Applies to the app and its information page (synthlucida.com/denik-vozidla-landing-en.html).

What VEHICLE LOGBOOK does

SYNTHLUCIDA VEHICLE LOGBOOK is a free online mileage log. You record individual trips by odometer reading, purpose (business, private, commute) and vehicle; the app keeps clear summaries and also lets you track vehicle service records (servicing, inspections, tyres, insurance, road-toll vignette). The whole app runs directly in your browser.

What data the app collects

The app doesn't collect or send any personal data to a server. Specifically:

  • There's no registration, login or user accounts.
  • No cookies, no Google Analytics and no advertising or tracking pixels.
  • No ads from ad networks — the only promoted links point to other SYNTHLUCIDA apps (such as RelaxPlayer).
  • The trips, odometer readings, driver names and service records you enter are processed entirely in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Visit statistics (GoatCounter)

For a basic overview of traffic we use GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-focused tool. It records the page visited, where the visitor came from (referrer), browser and OS type, screen size, language and approximate country.

It doesn't use cookies and doesn't store IP addresses. To recognize repeat page views within one visit, it keeps an anonymous hash of the IP address and browser for at most 8 hours. GoatCounter never sees your trips or service records.

Storage in your browser (localStorage)

The app stores the following in your browser's localStorage (locally on your device, not on a server):

  • denik_vozidla_entries_v1 — your saved trips (date, vehicle, purpose, route, odometer, driver, map link, note),
  • denik_vozidla_service_v1 — service records (type, date, vehicle, odometer, price and currency, next due date, note),
  • denik_vozidla_vehicles_v1 and denik_vozidla_current_vehicle_v1 — your vehicle list and the last selected vehicle,
  • denik_vozidla_state_v1 — the unfinished new-trip form, so it isn't lost when you close the page,
  • denik_vozidla_currency_v1 — the currency chosen for service prices,
  • denik_vozidla_install_snooze — the date you dismissed the home-screen install offer, so it doesn't show again right away.

The Czech and English versions of the app share the same data, so your records stay visible when you switch language. This data never leaves your browser. Remove it with the ✕ button next to an entry or vehicle, or by clearing the site's data in your browser settings — which deletes all your records, so make a backup first.

Data export and import

You can export your trips and service records as PDF or CSV (e.g. for tax purposes), or as a full JSON backup, which can later be imported back, for example when switching to a new device. Both export and import happen locally in your browser — the app never sends any of these files anywhere on its own; it only offers them for download, or processes a file you upload yourself.

External resources

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — the app's fonts. Loading the page sends a standard request to Google's servers.
  • GoatCounter (gc.zgo.at) — the script for the anonymous visit statistics described above.

As with any file loaded from the internet, these providers can technically see the IP address the request came from. Route map links (Google Maps, Mapy.cz) and links to other websites only open when you click them.

Accuracy and use for tax purposes

The app serves as supporting documentation for trips and servicing. For tax and accounting purposes we recommend backing up your records regularly (Complete backup → Export all) and consulting an accountant where needed.
