How it works

Four steps from bottles to a recipe

  1. Describe the e-liquid you want

    Volume in ml, target nicotine strength in mg/ml, and the PG/VG ratio the finished e-liquid should have.

  2. Add what you already have

    The strength and PG/VG ratio of your nicotine base from the label, plus the flavoring percentage. Choose whether the flavor is PG- or VG-based.

  3. The app works out the recipe

    See how many ml of base, flavoring, PG and VG to pour, the final ratio and an illustrated bottle. If a recipe can't be mixed, you'll see why.

  4. Save it

    Add the recipe to your list, download it as a PDF, or back it up to a file and move it to another device.

Worked example

How the app calculates – with real numbers

You want 30 ml of e-liquid at 3 mg/ml and 50/50. You have a 20 mg/ml (50/50) nicotine base and a flavor dosed at 10%.

  1. Nicotine base: 30 × 3 ÷ 20 = 4.5 ml
  2. Flavoring: 10% of 30 ml = 3 ml (counted as PG)
  3. A 50/50 target means 15 ml PG and 15 ml VG
  4. PG is already in the base (2.25 ml) and the flavor (3 ml), so add 15 − 2.25 − 3 = 9.75 ml PG
  5. VG is only in the base (2.25 ml), so add 15 − 2.25 = 12.75 ml VG

Recipe

  • Nicotine base4.5 ml
  • Flavoring3 ml
  • PG9.75 ml
  • VG12.75 ml

That's 30 ml at 50/50 and 3 mg/ml. Míchárna does exactly this for you — for any values you enter.

Features

What the app does

Accurate PG/VG ratio

Counts the PG and VG already in the base and flavoring and only adds what's missing.

Recipe check

Warns you when the target strength is higher than your base, or when the base alone holds more PG or VG than the target ratio allows.

PG- or VG-based flavors

An option for PG-based (most) or VG-based flavorings, or no flavoring at all.

PDF export

Backups

Data & privacy

What the app does and doesn't do

Where your data lives

  • Recipes are stored only in your browser (localStorage).
  • No server, no account, no login.
  • Your inputs and recipes are never sent anywhere.
  • We count visits with GoatCounter — no cookies, no stored IP addresses.
  • The jsPDF library is loaded from the cdnjs CDN for PDF export.
  • Move recipes between devices with a backup (JSON file).
Privacy policy →

Good to know

  • Results are approximate — only as accurate as the numbers on your labels and your measuring.
  • The calculator works in milliliters, not grams.
  • There's one field for flavoring — add up the percentages if you use several.
  • Recipes don't sync between devices. Without a backup, clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • The base and flavor manufacturers' instructions always come first.
  • For adults only. Nicotine base is toxic — wear gloves and keep it away from children and pets.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does Míchárna cost?

Nothing. It's free, with no paid features and no sign-up.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page and start calculating.

Does it work offline?

The calculation runs in your browser — once the page has loaded, it works without a connection. You need internet to open it the first time and for PDF export.

How do I move my recipes to another device?

In Saved recipes, click Download backup, then load the file on the other device with Upload backup. Existing recipes are kept; only new ones are added.

How do I add it to my home screen?

On Android in Chrome the app offers to install itself, or use the browser menu (Add to Home screen / Install app). On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.

How accurate is the calculation?

Results are rounded to hundredths of a milliliter. The real result depends on how accurate your labels are and how precisely you measure.

Does it work for nicotine salts?

Yes. The math is the same — just enter the strength and PG/VG ratio from the base label.

Is the app available in Czech?

Yes. The English and Czech versions share the same saved recipes.
