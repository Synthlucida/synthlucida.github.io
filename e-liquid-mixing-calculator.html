Guide — how to use Míchárna

Míchárna works out how many milliliters of nicotine base, flavoring, PG and VG to pour so the finished e-liquid has exactly the nicotine strength and PG/VG ratio you want. Everything is calculated right in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

1Describe the e-liquid you want

Target volume (ml)
How much e-liquid you want in total, e.g. 10, 30 or 60 ml.
Target nicotine strength (mg/ml)
How much nicotine the finished e-liquid should have. Enter 0 for nicotine-free.
Target PG/VG ratio
PG (propylene glycol) is thinner, carries flavor well and gives a stronger throat hit. VG (vegetable glycerin) is thicker, makes more vapor and feels smoother. Smaller pods often use 50/50; for bigger clouds, go higher on VG, e.g. 30/70.

2Enter what you already have

Nicotine base strength (mg/ml)
It's printed on the base label — most commonly 20 mg/ml.
Base PG/VG ratio
Also on the label. The calculator counts the PG and VG in the base, so the final ratio comes out right even when the base has a different ratio than your target.
Flavoring (% of volume)
The manufacturer lists the recommended dosage on the flavor bottle. Tick "no flavoring (base only)" to mix with just base, PG and VG.
Count flavoring as PG
Most flavor concentrates are PG-based, so this is on by default. Untick it if your flavor is VG-based.

3Calculate and pour

Click "Calculate the mix" (or press Enter in any number field). The cards show how many ml of each component to pour, the bottle shows the proportions, and the summary below it gives the total volume and final PG/VG ratio.

Changing a PG/VG ratio or a checkbox recalculates the recipe automatically. After you change a number, the result is grayed out until you click "Calculate the mix" again.

Example: 30 ml at 3 mg/ml and 50/50, from a 20 mg/ml (50/50) base, no flavoring:
  • nicotine base: 30 × 3 ÷ 20 = 4.5 ml
  • the base already contains 2.25 ml PG and 2.25 ml VG
  • to reach 15 ml PG and 15 ml VG, add 12.75 ml PG and 12.75 ml VG

4Save your recipe

  • Save this mix adds the recipe to Saved recipes. Use Load to put it back into the form, PDF to download it as a PDF and ✕ to delete it.
  • Export to PDF downloads the current recipe as a PDF, e.g. for printing.
  • Recipes are stored only in this browser. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, they won't be there — so download a backup now and then (Download backup) and restore it anywhere with Upload backup. Existing recipes are kept; only new ones are added.
  • The English and Czech versions share the same saved recipes.

!When the calculator shows an error

  • Target strength is higher than the base strength — a weaker base can't make a stronger e-liquid. Lower the target, or use a stronger base.
  • The base and flavoring take up more room than the total volume — lower the nicotine strength or the flavoring percentage.
  • The base alone contains more PG (or VG) than the target ratio allows — typical with a 100% PG base and a high-VG target. Pick a ratio with more PG, a base with more VG, or a lower nicotine strength.
  • One of the fields doesn't contain a valid number — make sure the fields contain only numbers. Negative values aren't accepted.

★Mixing tips

  • Use a separate syringe or measuring cup for each component so they don't mix.
  • VG is very thick and draws slowly — warming the bottle in lukewarm water for a moment helps.
  • After mixing, close the bottle and shake it well. Many flavors taste better after a few days of steeping.
  • Label the bottle with the nicotine strength and the date you mixed it.
  • On a phone, you can add Míchárna to your home screen from the browser menu and open it like an app.

Safety: concentrated nicotine base is toxic on skin contact and if swallowed. Wear gloves, label your bottles clearly and keep everything out of reach of children and pets. The calculator is approximate.

?FAQ

Is the e-liquid mixing calculator free?
Yes, the tool is completely free and runs directly in your browser — no registration needed.
How does the calculator work out the PG/VG ratio?
It accounts for the PG and VG already contained in the nicotine base and, if used, in the flavoring, then tops up the rest of the volume with pure PG and VG so the final ratio matches your chosen target.
What if my nicotine base already has a different PG/VG ratio than I want in the final mix?
The calculator flags it with a warning if the base (or flavoring) contains more PG or VG than your chosen target ratio allows — in that case, try a different target ratio or a different base.
Can I calculate a recipe without any flavoring?
Yes — just tick "no flavoring (base only)" and the calculator will work out the recipe using only nicotine base, PG and VG.
Is nicotine base dangerous?
Concentrated nicotine base is toxic on skin contact and if swallowed, so it's recommended to wear gloves and keep it out of reach of children and pets.

E-liquid mixing guides

Everything that matters for DIY mixing: what you need, how to choose a PG/VG ratio and nicotine strength, how much flavoring to use, how shortfills and longfills work, and how to store e-liquid safely.

1

What you need to mix e-liquid
  • Nicotine base — a PG/VG mix with nicotine, most commonly 20 mg/ml in 10 ml bottles. You don't need it for nicotine-free e-liquid.
  • Pure PG and VG — nicotine-free, used to top up the volume and set the ratio.
  • Flavoring — a concentrate made for e-liquids. Regular baking flavorings and essential oils don't belong in e-liquid.
  • Syringes or measuring cups — ideally one per component (e.g. 5 and 10 ml) so they don't get mixed up.
  • Empty bottles with child-resistant caps and dropper tips.
  • Nitrile gloves, paper towels, labels and a marker.
2

PG and VG: which ratio to choose

PG (propylene glycol) is thinner, carries flavor well and gives a stronger throat hit. VG (vegetable glycerin) is thicker, slightly sweet, makes more vapor and feels smoother. Ratios are written as PG/VG, so 30/70 means 30% PG and 70% VG.

  • 50/50 or 60/40 — MTL (mouth-to-lung, like a cigarette draw), small pods and clearomizers. Stronger flavor and throat hit.
  • 30/70 to 20/80 — DTL (direct-to-lung) and sub-ohm tanks. More vapor, smoother draw.

Thick, high-VG e-liquid wicks poorly in small pods with narrow openings, which can cause dry or burnt hits — stick closer to 50/50 for those. If PG irritates your throat, try a ratio with more VG.

3

Nicotine base and nicotine strength

Strength in mg/ml tells you how many milligrams of nicotine are in each milliliter of e-liquid — 3 mg/ml means 3 mg of nicotine in every ml.

  • Regular (freebase) nicotine gets harsher at higher strengths. DTL and sub-ohm vaping commonly uses 3–6 mg/ml.
  • Nicotine salts stay smoother even at higher strengths, which is why they're mostly used in small pods.
  • The calculator works for both — just enter the strength and PG/VG ratio from the base label.
  • Want to cut down gradually? Make each new batch a little weaker. VAPETRACK can help you keep track of how much you vape.
  • Nicotine base darkens over time when exposed to air and light (oxidation). Keep it tightly closed, cool and dark.
4

Flavorings and how much to use
  • The manufacturer lists the recommended dosage on the bottle, usually as a percentage — how many ml of flavor per 100 ml of e-liquid. For example, 10% in 30 ml means 3 ml of flavor.
  • Start at the lower end of the range. More flavor isn't always better — an over-flavored e-liquid can taste bitter or perfumey.
  • Mixing several flavors? Add up their percentages and enter the total in the Flavoring field (e.g. 5% + 3% = 8%).
  • Most flavorings are PG-based, which is why "count flavoring as PG" is on by default. Turn it off for VG-based flavors.
5

Shortfill and longfill (shake & vape)

Shortfill

A bottle of nicotine-free e-liquid with room left for a nicotine shot (booster). Typically 50 ml of e-liquid in a 60 ml bottle + one 10 ml, 20 mg/ml shot = 60 ml at roughly 3.3 mg/ml.

Formula: shot strength × shot ml ÷ total volume = 20 × 10 ÷ 60 = 3.33 mg/ml

Longfill

A larger bottle with a smaller amount of flavor (e.g. 12 ml in a 60 ml bottle) that you top up with base, PG and VG. In the calculator, enter the bottle size as the target volume and the flavor as a percentage: 12 ÷ 60 × 100 = 20%.

Shake the bottle well after topping it up. A shortfill is usually ready right away; a longfill usually needs a few days to steep.

6

Step-by-step mixing
  1. Set up a clean table with paper towels, and put on gloves.
  2. Work out the recipe in the calculator and save it (or download it as a PDF).
  3. Pour the nicotine base into an empty bottle.
  4. Add the flavoring.
  5. Add the PG, then the VG last — it's the thickest and pours the slowest.
  6. Close the bottle and shake it well for 1–2 minutes.
  7. Write the nicotine strength, PG/VG ratio and date on the label.
  8. Let it steep (see the next guide) and wash your hands when you're done.
7

Steeping

After mixing, the ingredients and flavors keep blending, so the taste usually improves and rounds out over a few days.

  • Fruit and menthol e-liquids are often ready quickly, in about 1–3 days.
  • Tobacco, cream and dessert flavors usually need 1–2 weeks.
  • Keep the bottle in the dark at room temperature and give it a shake once a day.
  • Flavor makers often list a recommended steep time right on the bottle.
8

Storage and safety
  • Store nicotine base and finished e-liquids in clearly labeled bottles with child-resistant caps, out of reach of children and pets — ideally in a closed cabinet.
  • Cool, dark storage extends shelf life. Keep bases and e-liquids out of direct sunlight.
  • Don't mix near food, and wash your hands afterwards.
  • If you spill some on your skin, wash it off right away with soap and water.

If swallowed or if you suspect poisoning, call your local emergency number or a poison control center (in the US: 1-800-222-1222).

9

Common problems and fixes
  • Weak flavor — let it steep longer, or add 1–2% more flavor next time.
  • Too harsh on the throat — lower the nicotine strength or pick a ratio with more VG.
  • Dry or burnt hits in a pod — thick, high-VG e-liquid wicks poorly in small pods; try 50/50.
  • Leaking tank — thin, high-PG e-liquid can leak from large sub-ohm tanks; try more VG.
  • E-liquid turned darker — normal with nicotine and some flavorings (oxidation). Store it in the dark.
  • The calculator shows an error — see the app guide for what each message means.
