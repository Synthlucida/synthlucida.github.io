What the app tracks

Feature overview

➕

Puff counter

One tap on the button adds 5 puffs. You see today's count and the time of your last puff. The "undo last entry" button removes the last addition if you tap by mistake.

📊

Last 14 days

A bar chart with the average marked, so it's easy to see how recent days compare to your usual count.

🗓️

Yearly heatmap

An overview of the last 365 days that scrolls sideways, so patterns over time are easier to spot.

🕐

Hourly distribution

Shows which hour of the day you puff most often, based on the last 7 days.

📉

Trend and gaps

A comparison of this week with last week in percent, plus the average, shortest, and longest gap between puffs (long overnight gaps are excluded).

🎯

Optional daily goal

The app just shows your status against a set limit with a colored progress bar — it doesn't judge or give advice.

🏆

XP and levels

A long-term system that calculates XP by comparing each day to your 7-day average — it rewards an actual downward trend, not individual days.

📋

Entry history

An editable list of every entry — individual puffs can be deleted after the fact too.

💾

Export and backup

Export to PDF and CSV, plus backup and restore in JSON format to move data between devices. The app also offers 5 color themes.

About the app

Why it was made

The idea for VAPETRACK came from personal experience with vaping. It's easy to lose track of how much you actually vape in a day — puffs happen almost automatically, without thinking, and nicotine consumption quietly creeps up.

VAPETRACK was built as a tool that tracks puffs and consumption in detail, so you can get a real picture of the habit. The app is entirely free, sends nothing to a server, and stores no personal data — everything stays in your browser until you download a backup yourself.

FAQ

What people usually ask

Is VAPETRACK free?

Yes, the app is entirely free and doesn't require registration or an account.

Where does the app store data?

Everything is calculated and stored right in this browser. The app doesn't send anything to a server.

What happens if I uninstall the app or clear my browser data?

Without a backup the history is lost. The app lets you back up your data to a JSON file at any time and restore it later from that same file.

Can the app be used on a phone?

Yes, the app is responsive and can be added to a phone's home screen as a shortcut.

Does the app give advice on quitting?

No. The app only shows numbers and charts, it doesn't give recommendations or judge on its own.

Can the app be switched to Czech?

Yes, the CZ/EN switch is near the top of the app.
