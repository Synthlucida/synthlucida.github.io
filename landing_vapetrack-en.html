How it works

Three steps, no sugar-coating

The app only counts and shows. No advice, no judging – the numbers speak for themselves.

01

Tap

Every time you vape, tap the big button – one tap logs 5 puffs. Hit it by mistake? The last entry can be undone with one tap.

02

Track

The app instantly recalculates today, your 7-day average, the comparison with yesterday and last week, the hours you vape most, and the gaps between puffs.

03

See the trend

The 14-day chart and the yearly heatmap show where your consumption is heading. XP and trophies only come from days below your usual average – a worse day never sets you back.

Privacy

Your data stays with you

VAPETRACK has no server for your entries. Everything is calculated right in your browser.

  • No sign-up. No account, email or login.
  • Only in this browser. Your entries live in your device’s localStorage and the app never sends them anywhere.
  • Backup in your hands. Download a JSON file and restore your data from it on any device.
  • No ads, no tracking. The site only counts visits anonymously via GoatCounter, with no cookies.

About

Why VAPETRACK was made

The idea for VAPETRACK came from personal experience with vaping. It’s easy to lose track of how much you actually vape in a day — puffs happen almost automatically, without thinking, and nicotine consumption quietly creeps up.

VAPETRACK was built as a tool that tracks puffs and consumption in detail, so you can get a real picture of the habit. The app is entirely free, never sends your entries anywhere, and stores no personal data — everything stays in your browser until you download a backup yourself.

FAQ

What people usually ask

Is VAPETRACK free?

Yes, the app is entirely free and doesn’t require registration or an account.

Where does the app store data?

Everything is calculated and stored right in this browser (localStorage). Your entries are never sent to a server.

What happens if I uninstall the app or clear my browser data?

Without a backup the history is lost. The app lets you back up your data to a JSON file at any time and restore it later from that same file.

Can the app be used on a phone?

Yes, the app is responsive and can be installed on a phone or computer as an app.

Do I have to install it?

No. VAPETRACK runs right in the browser; installing it on your home screen is just an optional shortcut.

What is the “Days off vaping” calendar for?

Mark a day there when you don’t want it measured – the app then leaves it out of averages, charts, the trend and XP. You can unmark it at any time.

Does the app give advice on quitting?

No. The app only shows numbers and charts, it doesn’t give recommendations or judge on its own.

Can the app be switched to Czech?

Yes, the CZ/EN switch is near the top of the app.
