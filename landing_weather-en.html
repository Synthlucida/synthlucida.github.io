Why it’s weather with a twist

Next to the temperatures and graphs, the app adds a short one-liner for every kind of weather – in plain, casual language rather than a dry textbook description. The lines change with the current conditions and the day of the week, and the app keeps count of how long is left until the weekend.

Clear

Not a single cloud up there. Grab your shades and go.

Rain

It’s bucketing down – an umbrella is mandatory kit today.

Heat

Hot as a sauna – drink water and don’t play the hero in the sun.

Frost

Cold as a freezer – dress in layers like an onion.

Fog

Fog as thick as soup – switch your lights on.

Storm

The sky’s putting on a free firework show, watch it from the window.

What it does

Everything that matters about the weather, in one place

From a quick glance at the temperature to a detailed hourly forecast – plus a few things you won’t find in the usual weather apps.

Current weather

Temperature, feels-like, humidity, precipitation, sunshine and wind, including gusts and a compass direction.

Hourly forecast

The next 24 hours with a clear temperature graph and the chance of rain for every hour.

7-day forecast

Tap a day to see precipitation, wind, UV index and sunrise and sunset times.

Severe weather warnings

Storms, strong wind, heavy rain, snow, ice, extreme heat or frost and high UV in the coming days.

48-hour rain outlook

When the rain starts and stops, roughly how much will fall and whether a storm is joining in.

Pressure & how you feel

Air pressure, its trend and how it may make you feel on the day.

What to wear

Clothing tips based on feels-like temperature, rain and wind – from shorts to full Siberia mode.

Where to go

Cycling, skating, hiking, camping, sledging and mushroom foraging – whether today works and when it gets better.

Sun & moon

An arc of the sun between sunrise and sunset, the moon phase and the next full and new moon.

Golden & blue hour

The best light for photos, calculated for today and your chosen place.

ISS flyovers

When the International Space Station will visibly fly over you, where it rises and how high it gets.

Favourites & export

Saved cities one tap away, colour themes and data export to PDF or CSV.

FAQ

What people ask most

Is SYNTHLUCIDA WEATHER free?

Yes, the app is completely free and needs no sign-up or account.

Where does the weather data come from?

The forecast is loaded in real time from Open-Meteo, which is based on models from national weather services.

Does it work for any city?

Yes, just type the name of any city in the world, or allow your location with one tap. Times are shown in the local time of the chosen city.

Can it warn me about dangerous weather?

Yes, it flags storms, strong wind, heavy rain or snow, ice, extreme heat or frost and high UV in the coming days. These aren’t official warnings from a national weather service, they’re calculated from the forecast.

Where does the app store data?

Favourite cities and your chosen colour theme stay only in your browser. The app has no server of its own, uses no cookies and only counts visits anonymously.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, it’s built mainly for mobile and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

Is there a Czech version?

Yes – the app was born in Czech, you’ll find the Czech version at weather.html.
