              About SYNTHLUCIDA Player

              Relax Player is a quiet place for ambient music — a continuous stream of ASMR, piano and chillout that you open in your browser and simply let play. No account, no sign-up, nothing to set up. It rests on one idea: listening should be effortless, and the screen can be as calming as the sound coming from it.

              Three moods, one stream. ASMR brings rain, waves, crackling fire and birdsong. Piano is slow and spacious, with room between every note. Chill adds warm synth pads and a gentle downtempo pulse. Switch between moods without interrupting the music, and the player remembers where you left off.

              Layer your own soundscape. In the ASMR playlist tap the layers button and pick a second ASMR track: it loops together with the first, and a small knob sets its volume separately. Rain with distant thunder, a stream with birdsong, a campfire under a night sky — mix until it sounds right and save the blend for next time. It also works in MY PLAYLIST and in your own playlists, as long as they contain an ASMR track.

              A look for every mood. Twenty-eight colour themes recolour the whole interface and its glow — from deep space and a starry night sky with a quietly shining moon to a warm sunset with flying birds. Live weather — sun, rain, snow and even a windier blizzard — can be laid over any theme, so the screen breathes along with the music.

              Breathing and sleep. Five guided breathing techniques — 4-7-8, Box, Coherent, Calm and Sigh — are shown as a slowly pulsing orb (or, if you prefer, a turning mandala) that you simply follow, and you can add a rhythm of your own. For moments when the body needs movement before breath, there are short stretches, and the TOGETHER room where everyone breathes 4-7-8 in the same second, with an evening group session at 21:00 Prague time. The sleep timer ends listening after 15, 30 or 60 minutes, and gentle reminders invite you to take a small pause during the day. Most of it runs locally on your device.

              Binaural beats. Put on stereo headphones and choose a wave — DELTA for deep rest, THETA for meditation or ALPHA for calm relaxation. Two slightly different pure tones, one in each ear, merge into a slow, gentle pulse. Separate knobs set the tones and the music, so you can lay a favourite track over them, and the sleep timer fades everything out once you fall asleep. It is an aid to relaxation, not a treatment.

              Your own listening. Add any track to My Playlist with the heart, reorder it, and export or import the whole playlist as a file. My Files lets you upload your own audio and mix it with the SYNTHLUCIDA catalogue in named playlists. Crossfade softens the transitions between tracks, History remembers what you listened to, and offline download keeps the music in the installed app even without a connection. Listening also quietly collects levels and experience, which gradually unlock track downloads.

              Small worlds to explore. Card of the Day for a short moment of reflection. An interactive night sky where you play a generative synthesizer, layer binaural waves and nature sounds, and watch the real phase of the moon. SYNTHLUCIDA BLOCKS, a falling-blocks game in which every cleared line unlocks more music. And a drawing canvas where parts of a picture can be brought to life — a wing that flaps, a star that twinkles.

              Relax Player is free, runs in any browser on phone and computer, and is also available for Windows in the Microsoft Store. It grows slowly and carefully, and every part of it comes from a sincere love of ambient and electronic sound. I wish you a few calm minutes — or a long, quiet evening. — Dave, SYNTHLUCIDA

              Playback

              Use the center button to play or pause the current track, and ⏭ to skip to the next one. Tap 🔀 to shuffle the playlist or 🔁 to repeat the current track. Drag the progress bar to seek to any point, and use the slider to adjust the volume. The 🔆 button underneath keeps your screen from dimming or locking while it's on, so playback stays visible during a session — tap it again to let your screen sleep normally. Browsing a different playlist than the one that's playing? Tap the 🔍 next to the time to jump straight back to the playing track - the main window switches to its playlist (or to MY PLAYLIST), scrolls to the track and makes it light up for a moment.

              Atmospheres & playlists

              Tap MUSIC at the top of the screen to open the ASMR, PIANO and CHILL playlists (the button shows the one you are on), and EXPLORE to open GAME, RELAX, DRAWING (separate pages with their own soundscapes), FORECAST (the SYNTHLUCIDA weather page, opened in a new tab) BREATHE (a guided breathing session), BINAURAL (calming binaural beats - see Binaural beats below) and SEARCH (find a track by name). The playlist you last listened to is remembered automatically the next time you open the player. Feeling like a surprise? Tap the small 🎲 to the left of MUSIC: it picks a completely random track from all playlists except ASMR, starts playing it and shows its playlist below. Shuffle then switches on (the 🎲 and 🔀 buttons light up), so when the track ends the player keeps picking random tracks from that playlist - tap 🔀 again to turn it off.

              Search

              Tap EXPLORE and then SEARCH to open the search window. Type a word in English or Czech - for example rain, déšť, piano, moře or noc - and matching tracks from ASMR, PIANO and CHILL appear as you type (capital letters and accents don't matter, and Czech words are matched to the English track names). Tap a track to jump straight to its playlist in the main window, where it scrolls into view and lights up for a moment, or tap the ▶ next to it to play it right away. Press Enter to open the first result. If no track contains all your words, the closest matches are shown.

NEON and AURORA shift gently over time on their own. At the bottom of the theme list, ◐ 2 COLORS lets you combine any two colours: pick COLOR 1 for the top of the player and COLOR 2 for the bottom, and the whole interface blends smoothly from one to the other. SWAP flips them, OFF returns to a single theme, and SAVE remembers your mix.

              Ambient effects

              WEATHER cycles through four states: a warm SUN scene with drifting light and birds crossing the sky, a soft falling RAIN animation, a quiet WINTER scene with gently falling snow, then a windier WINTER II blizzard with slanted, faster snow. SUNSET cycles through three states: a warm sunset gradient, a second sunset variant SUNSET II, then a living starry NIGHT sky with a softly glowing moon. Both WEATHER and SUNSET/NIGHT can be combined with any visual theme, and are remembered when you save your settings. WEATHER and SUNSET don't mix freely: SUN (your photos) runs alone, so switching it on turns SUNSET/NIGHT off and the other way round; RAIN, WINTER and WINTER II can be combined only with NIGHT, never with SUNSET or SUNSET II. While RAIN or WINTER is on, the SUNSET button therefore skips straight to NIGHT, and while NIGHT is on, the WEATHER button skips SUN. Tap ＋ PHOTO next to WEATHER to add your own photos to the SUN rotation — pick one or several from your device and they join in after the built-in sunset photos, stored only on your device.

              Binaural beats

              Tap EXPLORE and then BINAURAL to open a calm full-screen page for binaural beats - two slightly different pure tones, one in each ear, that your brain blends into a slow, gentle pulse. Put on stereo headphones (on speakers or in mono the effect disappears) and pick a wave: DELTA (2 Hz, deep rest and sleep), THETA (5 Hz, meditation) or ALPHA (9 Hz, calm relaxation); OFF turns the tones off. There are three knobs: TONES sets only the binaural tones (default 5 %), GAIN sets how loud TONES gets at 100 % (0 to +15 dB, default +12 dB) and MUSIC sets the music from the mini player (the same volume as in the main window). Keep the tones quieter than the music and raise them slowly. Drag a knob up or down, scroll over it or use the arrow keys, and double-tap to reset it. The mini player at the bottom works just like the one in BREATHE (ASMR, PIANO, CHILL, MY PLAYLIST and your own files), so you can layer music over the tones. Under the knobs you can set the sleep timer (15, 30 or 60 minutes, ✖ cancels it) - it is the same timer as in the main window, and when it ends the tones fade away and the music stops. The selector under the title switches between the ORB and an analog VU meter that shows the level of the TONES, of the MUSIC, of both together (MIX), or the RATIO of tones to music (needle to the left = music is louder, to the right = tones are louder). The ⓘ icon on the page opens a detailed guide. The tones also fade out when you leave the page with ✖. Binaural beats are a relaxation aid, not a treatment: do not use them while driving or when you must stay alert, and consult a doctor first if you have epilepsy, a heart condition, a pacemaker, a mental-health condition or are pregnant.

                How to breathe

                Sit comfortably and follow the glowing orb: it expands on the inhale, holds steady and shimmers during a hold, and shrinks on the exhale. Breathe through the nose when the technique doesn't say otherwise. Pick whichever technique fits the moment — you can switch anytime.

                inhale hold (full lungs) exhale hold (empty lungs)

                4-7-8 — Dr. Weil

                Inhale quietly through the nose for 4s, hold for 7s, then exhale completely through the mouth for 8s. Popularized by Dr. Andrew Weil, this ratio is widely used to ease anxiety and wind down before sleep by shifting the body toward a "rest and digest" state.

                Box — Navy SEAL method

                Inhale for 4s, hold for 4s, exhale for 4s, hold empty for 4s. An equal-count pattern used by Navy SEALs and first responders to stay calm and sharply focused under pressure.

                Coherent breathing

                Inhale and exhale evenly for about 5.5s each — roughly 5-6 breaths per minute. Studied as a resonance frequency in heart-rate variability (HRV) research, linked to better vagal tone and stress resilience over time.

                Calm — extended exhale

                Inhale for 4s, exhale for 6s, making the out-breath longer than the in-breath. A longer exhale stimulates the vagus nerve and slows the heart rate — a quick way to downshift after a stressful moment.

                Sigh — Huberman Lab

                Two inhales through the nose (one long, one short "sip" on top of it), followed by one long, slow exhale through the mouth. Studied at Stanford's Huberman Lab as one of the fastest ways to lower physiological stress in real time.

                Custom — your own rhythm

                Tap the pencil button to set your own inhale, hold and exhale lengths. Save it and it stays available here. Choose a rhythm that feels comfortable and don't strain.

                General relaxation information only — not medical advice. If you feel dizzy or unwell, breathe normally and stop.

                Stretches

                Light stretches you can do anywhere in a few minutes, no equipment needed. Pick one for the spot that feels most tense, or run through all five as a short routine — then come back to the breathing.

                1Neck stretch

                30–45 seconds

                Sit or stand up straight, shoulders relaxed away from your ears. Slowly tilt your head toward your right shoulder until you feel a gentle stretch on the left side of your neck. Hold for 15–20 seconds, then return slowly and switch sides.

                Releases tension in the neck — one of the first places stress settles.

                2Shoulder rolls

                20–30 seconds

                Let your arms hang loosely at your sides. Slowly roll your shoulders backward in big, slow circles — up, back, down and forward. After 5–8 rounds, reverse the direction.

                Gets blood flowing through the upper back and helps drop tension from hunched shoulders.

                3Standing forward fold

                30–60 seconds

                Stand with feet hip-width apart. Slowly bend forward from the hips, knees softly bent, letting your head and arms hang loosely toward the floor. Let your head and neck fully relax, breathing calmly.

                Stretches the back and neck — the inverted head position briefly calms the nervous system.

                4Shaking out hands & legs

                20–30 seconds

                Stand up and gently shake out your hands from the wrists, as if flicking off water, then add your whole arms. Next, balance on one leg and shake out the other, then switch sides.

                A fast physical reset that helps discharge built-up nervousness and adrenaline.

                5Knees to chest

                30–60 seconds

                Lie on your back (or sit), pull both knees toward your chest and gently hug them with your arms. Let your head rest, relaxed, and breathe slowly into your belly.

                Gently stretches the lower back and creates a sense of safety, similar to a curled-up resting position.

                Tip: they work best back to back as a short 2–3 minute routine, followed right away by a few minutes of guided breathing — first release the body, then calm your breath and mind.

                General relaxation information only — not medical advice. Stop if anything hurts or you feel unwell.
