Workflow

How it works

Five steps between a blank page and a finished, fine-tuned foundation for your song.

1

Pick a key and a mood

The key sets the root note; the mood decides major or minor, the tempo, and the overall character of the progression.

2

The tool builds the chords

The progression is built solely from chords diatonic to the chosen key — no stray or random notes, always a consistent sound.

3

Melody, bass, arp and drums are added

Pick a specific style for each layer, or leave it on "auto" and get a different variation every time.

4

Fine-tune it in the Mini DAW

Balance each layer's volume in the mixer, then open the Mini DAW to mute individual steps, shift notes up or down an octave, or re-roll just one bar that isn't working.

5

Play it, or export it

Loop it right in your browser, download the MIDI file for your DAW, or export the progression as sheet music (PDF) — no license restrictions attached, and every edit you made is baked in.

FAQ

What people usually ask

What exactly is Progrese?

A free web-based chord progression generator. Pick a key and mood and it builds a musically coherent 8-bar progression with melody, bassline and arpeggio, which you can play in your browser or download as MIDI.

Do I need to install anything?

No — Progrese runs entirely in your web browser, with nothing to install and no sign-up. Just open the page on your computer or phone.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, the interface is fully responsive and adapts to phone, tablet and desktop screens.

Is the MIDI export free even for commercial music?

Yes. Any MIDI file you generate and download is yours to use freely, in personal or commercial music, with no license fees and no attribution required.

Which DAWs is the export compatible with?

The export is a standard MIDI file (.mid, format 1), compatible with Ableton, FL Studio, Logic, Reaper, Cubase and more.

How many moods can I choose from?

Eight: happy, sad, dreamy, epic, chill/lo-fi, dark, romantic and nostalgic — each with its own key mode, tempo and set of typical progressions.

Can I adjust the volume of each instrument separately?

Yes. The built-in mixer lets you set the volume of chords, melody, bass, arp and drums independently, so you can tuck a layer back or bring it forward before you play or export.

Can I edit the notes after generating a progression?

Yes — that's what the Mini DAW panel is for. Pick any bar and mute individual melody, bass, arp or drum steps, shift a note up or down an octave, or re-roll just that one bar. Every change updates playback and the MIDI export immediately.

Can I export the progression as sheet music?

Yes — the "Sheet music (PDF)" button exports the current progression as standard music notation (treble and bass clef, chord symbols above the staff) into a PDF you can print or send along. Below the notation, each bar also gets a piano key diagram with the keys marked, so it works even if you can't read notes. It reflects any edits made in the Mini DAW too.

Can I hear the progression through my own synth in a DAW, without exporting a MIDI file?

Yes — the "Live to MIDI" panel sends playback in real time to a virtual MIDI port. Set up a virtual port on your computer (loopMIDI is free on Windows; the built-in IAC Driver works on Mac), point an instrument track in your DAW at it with any synth loaded, then enable MIDI access in the app and turn on "Live sending". Works in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox — unfortunately Safari doesn't support the Web MIDI API.
