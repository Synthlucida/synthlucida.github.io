CARD OF THE DAY

THE DRAW

Every time you open this page, one card is drawn truly at random from the full 78-card tarot deck — the 22 Major Arcana and the 56 Minor Arcana — and shown either upright or reversed.

TWO DECKS

Switch anytime between the classic 78-card Tarot and the original 24-card Cosmic Whisper Oracle using the tabs below the title. The very first card each time you open this page is always drawn from a randomly chosen deck.

THE DECK

The Major Arcana (The Fool through The World) speak to big life themes and turning points. The Minor Arcana are grouped into four suits — Wands, Cups, Swords and Pentacles — each with its own flavor: passion and action, emotion and intuition, thought and conflict, or the material world and the body. The Oracle deck carries its own single, simpler message per card, with no reversed meanings.

UPRIGHT & REVERSED

An upright card points to its meaning flowing freely. A reversed card (marked REVERSED) suggests that same energy turned inward, blocked, or asking for patience.

DRAW AGAIN

Tap 🔀 DRAW AGAIN any time for a fresh card, or simply reopen this page later.

A GENTLE NOTE

This is offered as a small daily moment for reflection and atmosphere, not as prediction or advice.
