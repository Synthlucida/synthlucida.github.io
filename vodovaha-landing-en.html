How it works

Four steps to an accurate reading

  1. Open the app

    All you need is a browser. On iPhone you just allow access to the motion sensors.

  2. Pick a mode

    Surface – lay the phone down. Angle – lay it down or stand it on its edge like a classic level.

  3. Calibrate zero

    Once, on a surface you know is level. The app remembers the calibration.

  4. Read and save

    When level, the bubble turns green and the app beeps. Save a reading with a label and export it to CSV.

Worked example

How the app converts a slope – with real numbers

A driveway or ramp that the app measures at 5°. What does that look like in other units?

  1. Slope in percent: tan(5°) × 100 = 8.7 %
  2. Ratio: 1 : (1 ÷ tan 5°) = 1 : 11.4
  3. Rise over 2 m: 2,000 mm × 0.0875 = 175 mm
  4. In gradians: 5 × 400 ÷ 360 = 5.56 gon
  5. You'll find the same conversions in the app's Convert tab – “Use live angle” inserts the current reading.

Result

  • Angle5.0°
  • Slope8.7 %
  • Ratio1 : 11.4
  • Rise over 2 m175 mm

The app also shows the slope in percent right under the reading in Angle mode.

Tips

How to measure as accurately as possible

A few habits that get the most out of your phone:

🎯

Calibrate on a true level

Set zero on a surface you know is level – for example checked with a classic spirit level.

📱

Mind the case and camera bump

A camera bump or case tilts the phone. Always measure the same way and calibrate in the same case.

🔄

Turn it 180°

Measure, turn the phone 180° on the same spot and measure again. The average cancels out the phone's own error.

⏱️

Let the phone settle

The value settles within a fraction of a second – don't read it while the phone is moving.

📏

Calibrate the ruler too

Without calibration it's approximate. With a bank card you set it to the millimeter.

🧱

Double-check on site

For construction and professional work, always verify with a classic spirit level.

Data & privacy

What the app does and doesn't do

Where your data lives

  • Sensor data is processed only in your browser and never sent anywhere.
  • The camera is used only to turn on the flash; no image is stored.
  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • Saved readings and calibration stay on your device only (localStorage).
  • We count visits with GoatCounter – no cookies, no stored IP addresses.
  • Download your saved readings as CSV.

Good to know

  • Accuracy depends on your phone's sensors – usually tenths of a degree to one degree.
  • The ruler only measures objects that fit on the screen.
  • On iPhone the screen lights up instead of the flash – the web can't control it.
  • Computers usually have no motion sensors – only demo mode works there.
  • The app is not a certified measuring instrument.
  • For construction, double-check with a classic spirit level.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does Spirit Level cost?

Nothing. It's free, with no paid features and no sign-up.

How does a digital level in the browser work?

It reads your phone's gyroscope and accelerometer and converts the data to a tilt angle in real time. It runs right in your browser, no app install needed.

Why do I have to allow sensors on iPhone?

Safari requires it for security reasons. Just tap “Enable sensors” and confirm the system prompt.

Does it work on a computer?

Computers usually don't have motion sensors. You can try the interface in demo mode with a slider; for measuring, use your phone.

How accurate is a web-based level?

Usually within tenths of a degree to one degree, depending on the sensors. It's most accurate after calibrating zero on a surface you know is level.

Can I measure the slope of a ramp or roof?

Yes. Angle mode shows the angle in degrees and the slope in percent below it. The 1 : n ratio is in the Convert tab.

How accurate is the on-screen ruler?

Only approximate until you calibrate it. Tap Calibrate, hold a bank card upright against the screen and match the frame to its 85.6 mm length – the app remembers it.

Can I add it to my home screen?

Yes. On Android in Chrome the app offers to install itself; on iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.
