Privacy Policy

App: SYNTHLUCIDA LEVEL · synthlucida.com/vodovaha-en.html · Last updated: Sep 24, 2026
Applies to the app and its information page (synthlucida.com/vodovaha-landing-en.html).

What LEVEL does

SYNTHLUCIDA LEVEL is a free web-based spirit level and inclinometer. The app reads your phone's gyroscope and accelerometer (DeviceOrientation API) and converts the data to a tilt angle in real time. It has a Surface mode for two-axis leveling, an Angle mode for measuring tilt, an on-screen ruler, a flashlight, a unit converter and a calculator. Everything runs directly in your browser, with no installation.

What data the app collects

The app doesn't collect or send any personal data to a server. Specifically:

  • There's no registration, login or user accounts.
  • No cookies, no Google Analytics and no advertising or tracking pixels.
  • No ads from ad networks — the only promoted links point to other SYNTHLUCIDA apps (such as RelaxPlayer).
  • Sensor readings (tilt, angle) are processed entirely in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Sensor and camera access

The app needs access to your gyroscope and accelerometer (DeviceOrientation API) to show the device's tilt in real time — without it the level can't work. On iPhone and iPad the app asks for it with the “Enable sensors” button.

For the flashlight, on some devices (Android/Chrome) the app asks for camera access in order to turn on the flash. The camera image is never displayed, stored or sent anywhere — the camera is used only to control the flash and is released as soon as you leave the flashlight or send the app to the background. Where the flash can't be controlled (e.g. iPhone), the app lights up the screen instead.

Visit statistics (GoatCounter)

For a basic overview of traffic we use GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-focused tool. It records the page visited, where the visitor came from (referrer), browser and OS type, screen size, language and approximate country.

It doesn't use cookies and doesn't store IP addresses. To recognize repeat page views within one visit, it keeps an anonymous hash of the IP address and browser for at most 8 hours. GoatCounter never sees your measurements.

Storage in your browser (localStorage)

The app stores the following in your browser's localStorage (locally on your device, not on a server):

  • vodovaha_readings_en_v1 — readings you save with the “Save reading” button,
  • vodovaha_zero_v1 — the zero calibration of the level and inclinometer,
  • vodovaha_ruler_ppmm_en_v1 — the ruler calibration for your screen,
  • vodovaha_install_snooze — the date you dismissed the home-screen install offer, so it doesn't show again right away.

This data never leaves your browser. Delete saved readings with the ✕ button, the calibration with “Reset calibration”, or everything at once by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

Data export

You can export your saved readings as a CSV file. The export is created right in your browser — the app never sends it anywhere; the file downloads directly to your device.

External resources

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — the app's fonts. Loading the page sends a standard request to Google's servers.
  • GoatCounter (gc.zgo.at) — the script for the anonymous visit statistics described above.

As with any file loaded from the internet, these providers can technically see the IP address the request came from. Links to other websites only open when you click them.
