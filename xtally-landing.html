Frequently Asked Questions

Is Xtally really free?

Yes, Xtally Documents, Xtally Sheets and Xtally Scanner are all completely free, with no ads and no sign-up or account required.

Where are my documents and spreadsheets stored?

Everything stays in your browser, on your own device. The apps have no server or database, so nothing is ever uploaded.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. After the first load you can install the app as a PWA (Progressive Web App) and keep using it even without an internet connection.

Can the apps open Word and Excel files?

Yes — Xtally Documents can import and export .docx files for Microsoft Word, and Xtally Sheets can import and export .xlsx files for Microsoft Excel.

Does Xtally include a document scanner?

Yes — Xtally Scanner lets you scan documents with your camera or from uploaded photos, automatically crops and straightens the page, and combines everything into a single PDF.

Is Xtally related to SYNTHLUCIDA?

Yes, all three apps are standalone projects from the SYNTHLUCIDA family of free web tools.
